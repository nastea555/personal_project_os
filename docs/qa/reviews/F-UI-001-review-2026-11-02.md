## 1. Summary

F-UI-001 is the app shell: wide/narrow layouts with side navigation or a drawer, six routes plus 404, design tokens and base components, honest "В разработке · Этап N" stubs and Dashboard cards, and a health check that shows an error banner. v0.3 is much tighter than v0.2 (glossary, icon table, normative screen schematics, Back closes the drawer). One High finding remains: decision D-8 (localhost only) contradicts the product brief, where the owner uses the app every day from a phone. The spec's own phone checks (TC-UI-032, the phone Back gesture in AC-UI-024) depend on how that is resolved. The Medium findings are mostly gaps in the new detail: landmarks that clash with the layout definitions, health-check timing and the «Повторить» button, focus and history behaviour, which texts and sizes in §7 are binding and with what tolerance, and how the two NFRs are measured.

Not a requirements defect, but it also blocks implementation: the spec itself states that the build and test environment (ADR-009) is not chosen yet.
